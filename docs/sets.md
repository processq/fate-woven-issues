> **More to come: Set bonuses**
> Wear more pieces of a set and the set bonuses unlock. Full-set bonuses are held back for you to discover.

Rune sets give bonuses when you carry several pieces of one set. Set pieces drop from specific enemies and bosses.

## Stag-Thunder (Lightning, Meadows)

**Stag Rune** (element rune): Shocked enemies are also Staggered by your next hit. *Source: Trader quest in the Meadows.*

- **Antler Tine** (normal slot): Shocked from this ability arcs to 1 extra enemy when it is used up. *Source: 2-star Deer.*
- **Boar Bristle** (normal slot): Shocked lasts 1s longer. *Source: 2-star Boar.*
- **Neck Scale** (normal slot): +5% move speed while a Shocked enemy is within 10m. *Source: 2-star Neck.*
- **Stag's Leap** (Epic slot): to be discovered. *Source: Eikthyr.*
- **Eikthyr's Crown** (Legendary slot): to be discovered. *Source: Eikthyr.*

## Greydwarf Grove (Nature, Black Forest)

**Root Rune** (element rune): When a Root from this ability ends, the enemy is Slowed 30% for 2s. *Source: Trader quest in the Black Forest.*

- **Greydwarf Bark** (normal slot): Root lasts 1s longer. *Source: Burial Chambers.*
- **Shaman's Herb** (normal slot): You heal 1 HP a second while an enemy you Rooted is within 10m. *Source: Greydwarf Shaman.*
- **Troll Sinew** (normal slot): Roots from this ability also catch 1 more enemy within 3m (the spread doesn't spread again). *Source: Troll.*
- **Troll Hide** (Epic slot): to be discovered. *Source: The Elder.*
- **Elder's Heart** (Legendary slot): to be discovered. *Source: The Elder.*

## Bog Rot (Poison, Swamp)

**Ooze Rune** (element rune): Venom stacks to 15 instead of 10. *Source: Trader quest in the Swamp.*

- **Leech Tooth** (normal slot): +1 Venom per hit. *Source: Sunken Crypts.*
- **Wraith Shroud** (normal slot): Venom lasts 2s longer. *Source: Wraith.*
- **Abomination Root** (normal slot): Each Venom stack also cuts the enemy's healing by a further 2%. *Source: Abomination.*
- **Draugr Rot** (Epic slot): to be discovered. *Source: Bonemass.*
- **Bonemass's Gift** (Legendary slot): to be discovered. *Source: Bonemass.*

## Drake's Winter (Frost, Mountains)

**Drake Rune** (element rune): When Frozen from this ability ends, the enemy keeps 2 Chilled. *Source: Trader quest in the Mountains.*

- **Wolf Fang** (normal slot): +1 Chill per hit. *Source: Frost caves.*
- **Golem Crystal** (normal slot): Chilled from this ability lasts 2s longer. *Source: Stone Golem.*
- **Fenring Claw** (normal slot): Frozen lasts 0.5s longer. *Source: Fenring.*
- **Silver Edge** (Epic slot): to be discovered. *Source: Moder.*
- **Moder's Breath** (Legendary slot): to be discovered. *Source: Moder.*

## Lox Plains (Earth, Plains)

**Lox Rune** (element rune): Staggered enemies are knocked back 2m. *Source: Trader quest in the Plains.*

- **Fuling Totem** (normal slot): +15% stagger. *Source: Fuling villages.*
- **Lox Pelt** (normal slot): +10% stagger. *Source: 2-star Lox.*
- **Growth Tar** (normal slot): Staggered enemies move 20% slower for 2s. *Source: Growth.*
- **Lox Horn** (Epic slot): to be discovered. *Source: Yagluth.*
- **Yagluth's Hand** (Legendary slot): to be discovered. *Source: Yagluth.*

## Dvergr Mist (Arcane, Mistlands)

**Dvergr Rune** (element rune): About 12 times a minute, a hit restores 3 eitr to you and every ally within 8m. *Source: Trader quest in the Mistlands.*

- **Seeker Chitin** (normal slot): +5% eitr regen while slotted. *Source: Infested Mines.*
- **Gjall Sac** (normal slot): Kills with this ability restore 5 eitr. *Source: Gjall.*
- **Dvergr Rune-Shard** (normal slot): This ability costs 10% less eitr. *Source: Dvergr Mage.*
- **Dvergr Lantern** (Epic slot): to be discovered. *Source: The Queen.*
- **Queen's Eye** (Legendary slot): to be discovered. *Source: The Queen.*

## Ember Ash (Fire, Ashlands)

**Ember Rune** (element rune): Burning stacks to 80% instead of 60%. *Source: Trader quest in the Ashlands.*

- **Charred Bone** (normal slot): Burning deals 10% more. *Source: Charred fortresses.*
- **Valkyrie Feather** (normal slot): Burning lasts 1s longer. *Source: Fallen Valkyrie.*
- **Morgen Ember** (normal slot): Burning from this ability adds 15% per re-application instead of 10% (still up to its cap). *Source: Morgen.*
- **Asksvin Scale** (Epic slot): to be discovered. *Source: Fader.*
- **Fader's Crown** (Legendary slot): to be discovered. *Source: Fader.*

## Tide-Wrack (Water, Ocean)

**Wave Rune** (element rune): Wet from this ability splashes onto enemies within 3m of the target. *Source: Haldor's quest: bring 3 serpent scales.*

- **Drowned Pearl** (normal slot): Hits from this ability refresh Wet on the target to its full length. *Source: Rare catch when fishing the Ocean (about 5%).*
- **Barnacle Shell** (normal slot): Wet lasts 2s longer. *Source: Mined from a Leviathan (about 5%).*
- **Serpent Fin** (normal slot): Swimming costs 10% less stamina and is 10% faster. *Source: Sea Serpents (about 5%).*
- **Serpent's Coil** (Epic slot): to be discovered. *Source: 2-star Sea Serpents (25%).*
- **Serpent King's Tooth** (Legendary slot): to be discovered. *Source: 2-star Sea Serpents (10%).*

## Night-Veil (Shadow, Swamp and Mistlands (crypts and ruins))

**Dusk Rune** (element rune): Shadow sneak attacks from this ability deal x8 instead of x6. *Source: The Bog Witch's quest.*

- **Wraith Shroud-Thread** (normal slot): Stealth from this ability lasts 1s longer. *Source: Wraiths (about 5%).*
- **Draugr Hood** (normal slot): Enemies that lose track of you search for you 2s longer before they can notice you again. *Source: Sunken Crypt chests (about 15%).*
- **Shade-Lantern Shard** (normal slot): +10% move speed while you're Stealthed. *Source: Dvergr ruin chests in the Mistlands (about 15%).*
- **Rancid Shroud** (Epic slot): to be discovered. *Source: Rancid Remains and 2-star Draugr Elites (25%).*
- **Night-Veil Crown** (Legendary slot): to be discovered. *Source: Rancid Remains and 2-star Draugr Elites (10%).*

## Bloodsworn (Blood, Swamp and Mountains)

**Blood Rune** (element rune): This ability heals you for 5% of the damage it deals to Bleeding enemies. *Source: Hildir's quest.*

- **Draugr Fang** (normal slot): Bleeding from this ability adds 15% per re-application instead of 10% (still up to 80%). *Source: Draugr (about 5%).*
- **Ulv Fang** (normal slot): Bleeding lasts 1s longer. *Source: Ulvs (about 5%).*
- **Bat Wing** (normal slot): Hits heal you for 2% of the damage dealt. *Source: Frost-cave bats (about 5%).*
- **Elite's Blood-Torc** (Epic slot): to be discovered. *Source: 2-star Draugr Elites and the frost-cave Cultist (25%).*
- **Cultist's Heart** (Legendary slot): to be discovered. *Source: 2-star Draugr Elites and the frost-cave Cultist (10%).*

## Ironforged (Steel, Plains and Mistlands (Fuling villages and Dvergr forges))

**Anvil Rune** (element rune): This ability's hits ignore 10% of the target's physical resistance. *Source: Haldor's quest: bring 10 black metal scrap.*

- **Fuling Blade-Chip** (normal slot): Enemies this ability Staggers also take Steel's light Bleeding. *Source: Fuling Berserkers (about 5%).*
- **Dvergr Rivet** (normal slot): Bleeding lasts 1s longer. *Source: Dvergr Rogues (about 5%).*
- **Forge Slag** (normal slot): +10% stagger damage. *Source: Fuling village chests (about 15%).*
- **Berserker's Iron Band** (Epic slot): to be discovered. *Source: 2-star Fuling Berserkers and 2-star Fuling Shamans (25%).*
- **Shaman's Forge-Crown** (Legendary slot): to be discovered. *Source: 2-star Fuling Berserkers and 2-star Fuling Shamans (10%).*

## Valhalla's Light (Holy, Black Forest, Swamp and Ashlands (the undead))

**Dawn Rune** (element rune): Marked from this ability spreads to 1 enemy within 5m when it lands (the spread doesn't spread again). *Source: Hildir's quest.*

- **Grave-Light Bone** (normal slot): Allies hitting an enemy Marked by this ability heal 3% of the damage dealt instead of 2%. *Source: Burial Chamber skeletons (about 5%).*
- **Draugr Ward-Stone** (normal slot): Marked lasts 1s longer. *Source: Draugr (about 5%).*
- **Charred Relic** (normal slot): +15% damage to undead. *Source: Charred warriors in the Ashlands (about 5%).*
- **Hero's Ring** (Epic slot): to be discovered. *Source: Any boss rematch (25%).*
- **Valkyrie's Wing** (Legendary slot): to be discovered. *Source: Any boss rematch from Moder onwards (10%).*
